Group Meeting Scheduler

Team members mark the times they are free each week. The app finds the slots where everyone is available and ranks the best ones, so groups stop going back and forth in chat.

Textbook Swap Board

Students list textbooks they want to sell or swap, and search by course code or title. Buyers can send a request, and sellers mark the book as sold when it is gone.

Node.jsExpressPostgreSQL
